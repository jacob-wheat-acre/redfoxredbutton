My workouts are not showing up

The app reads workouts from Apple Health. Check that it is allowed to: Settings, Apps, Health, Data Access & Devices, Fitness Hacker, and turn on Workouts, Steps, Active Energy, Distance and Heart Rate. Then open the app; workouts since you last opened it come in. A workout can also be logged by hand: on Home, tap the day.

I bought Full Route on another device

On Home, tap ⋯, then Full Route…, then Restore Purchases.

Notifications

The app tells you when a workout arrives and on the days you planned to train. Turn them on or off in Settings, Notifications, Fitness Hacker.
